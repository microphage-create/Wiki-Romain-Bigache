## Context

Every year, Danone IT launches its global satisfaction survey across 50+ countries. The problem: historically low participation rates. Employees see it as another corporate checkbox with no visible outcome or follow-up. Previous campaigns relied on institutional messaging ("Your opinion matters!") that everyone scrolls past. The brief: break the codes of internal comms and make people actually want to respond, in bilingual FR/EN.

## Approach

Internet meme hijacking (Kermit at the window, dog in the fire) paired with relatable IT frustration quotes. "My VPN on a Monday morning." "When the video call freezes right when you're talking." Email sequence with escalating absurdity to chase non-respondents. Manager toolkit with ready-to-post content. A tone of humor and self-deprecation unprecedented in corporate internal comms, deployed across Workplace and Viva Engage.

## Deliverables

- IT meme series for internal channels
- Bilingual posts FR/EN, localised humour
- Reminder emails with rising absurdity
- Manager kit: ready-to-post content
- Workplace and Viva Engage visuals

## Highlight

> When corporate comms switch from "please fill out the survey" to "your VPN on a Monday morning", participation follows.
