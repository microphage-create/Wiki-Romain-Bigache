## Context

Enedis IT department (5,500 employees, France's electricity network operator) needs to rally its teams behind Move To Cloud, an infrastructure migration programme affecting the entire organisation. After 2 years of conventional comms, fatigue has set in. Teams only see the constraints of change, not the benefits. The brief: reboot the communication to recapture attention in physical workspaces where posters compete with coffee machines.

## Approach

A series of contrarian posters: "The cloud won't water this plant*", "The cloud won't dry your hands*", "The cloud won't turn water into wine*". Each asterisk reveals a real, concrete benefit. Posters are placed as guerrilla pieces in the exact spaces they reference: next to the potted plant, in the restrooms, by the water cooler. Each has its own colour (green, blue, magenta) and custom yellow line-art illustration, extended to digital screens and the intranet.

## Deliverables

- Contrarian asterisk poster concept
- 8 posters with tricolour palette
- Guerrilla deployment: plants, fountains, restrooms
- Custom yellow line-art illustrations
- Digital screen and intranet adaptations

## Highlight

> The best way to explain something invisible is to point at something everyone can see. The plant. The water cooler. The hand dryer.
