## Context

JJA, a French consumer goods distributor (1,200 employees, 3 billion products per year), completed a 3-year IT transformation covering cybersecurity (EDR, WAF, SOC), data migration (Snowflake, PowerBI) and infrastructure modernisation (Nutanix). Outside IT, nobody measures the scale of this work. Dual challenge: make the technical teams proud of what they built, and demonstrate tangible value to business units who only see acronyms.

## Approach

Taking the most intimidating IT jargon (EDR, WAF, SOC, Nutanix, Snowflake) and setting it in multicoloured, kindergarten-style hand-drawn typography. The clash between technical complexity and childlike aesthetics stops people mid-scroll. "Sounds a bit abstract?" bridges into plain-language explanations. The visual system works for dual audiences: IT teams who feel seen, and business directors who finally understand. Tagline: "Together, our passion has a future."

## Deliverables

- Childlike typo / tech jargon concept
- Visual series by IT project
- Dual-audience DSI + business comms
- Executive committee presentation assets
- Intranet and event adaptations

## Highlight

> If a five-year-old can't read it, it's not jargon, it's a secret language. We turned it into crayon.
