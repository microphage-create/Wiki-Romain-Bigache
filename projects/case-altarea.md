## Context

Altarea, one of France's leading real estate groups (3,500 employees across development, investment and asset management), rolls out Microsoft Copilot 365 for all departments. The workforce has no tech culture: roles span property managers, project directors, legal, finance and marketing. Previous digital tool launches met with indifference or anxiety. The brief: make AI adoption feel exciting, not threatening, while speaking the language people already understand.

## Approach

Repurposing Altarea's own real estate vocabulary to describe an AI tool. "Bright, even brilliant, available immediately." "Treat yourself to an unobstructed view of your productivity." "Rococopilot style. Amateurs only." Each poster has its own colour (coral, blue, golden yellow) and a geometric architectural illustration tying the campaign to the Group's core business. Tech jargon is replaced by property listing language.

## Deliverables

- Real estate vocabulary applied to AI concept
- 3-poster launch (coral, blue, gold)
- Custom architectural illustrations
- Teaser sequence for internal channels
- Town Hall assets and screen wallpapers

## Highlight

> When you sell square metres for a living, the best way to explain AI is to list it like a property.
