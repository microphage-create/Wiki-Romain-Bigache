## Short description

Claria is the white-label declination of the gamified AI literacy product line started with [Altaria](./altaria.md): a branded, playable demo built for an enterprise prospect in the automotive services sector. Same product core (activity-based curriculum, quizzes, games, Duolingo-style progression), rethemed end to end to the prospect's brand: colors, illustrations, reward system, flat 3D tile icons.

The curriculum (including the Copilot track) predates the prospect's request: Claria demonstrates how an existing, proven product adapts to a client brand, not a bespoke build.

## What it demonstrates

- **White-label architecture**: one product core, full brand overlay without forking the codebase
- **Solo end-to-end delivery**: concept, code, design, content and brand assets in one person
- **AI-assisted asset pipeline**: brand-matched illustrations and 3D-styled icons produced with a controlled generation and cutout pipeline

## Stack

Next.js 15, Vercel AI SDK (Anthropic + OpenAI), dedicated Supabase instance.
