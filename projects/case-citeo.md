## Context

Citeo, the French eco-organisation for packaging and paper recycling (funded by 30,000+ companies), needs to raise its internal phishing resilience score from 6/10 to 7/10. Constraints: a tight budget, a C-suite not yet on board with cybersecurity awareness, and a workforce of 250 people across multiple offices already exposed to generic "don't click suspicious links" messaging. The brief demands a concept strong enough to work on walls with zero media spend.

## Approach

"The Hackers" concept: every poster is signed by the hackers themselves, thanking employees for their bad habits. "Thanks for ignoring Albert on Teams." "Thanks for leaving everything open. Your computer. Your session. Your heart, maybe." "Thanks for never updating your apps." The sarcasm grabs attention, the practical advice follows below. Art direction: pastel backgrounds, white card with custom icon, bold editorial serif type.

## Deliverables

- "The Hackers" campaign concept
- 5 hacker-signed awareness posters
- Guerrilla stickers and corridor signage
- Multi-site digital screen adaptations
- Cyber tone of voice guidelines

## Highlight

> When the hackers themselves write your internal comms, nobody scrolls past.
