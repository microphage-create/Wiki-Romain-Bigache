## Context

Danone (100,000+ employees, 55 countries) needs a high-impact campaign for European Cyber Month (October). Phishing and smishing attacks have increased, but previous awareness campaigns relied on corporate tone and compliance language that employees skip. The IT security team wants to reboot vigilance without sounding like a lecture, in a company where people sell yoghurt, not firewalls.

## Approach

Riddle-format posters with the answer printed upside down, forcing physical interaction. Bilingual wordplay ("What's the worst thing about a password? Lacking character.", "She URL"). Custom illustrations and "Be Cyber Safe" identity. Guerrilla deployment in cafeterias, printers, open spaces. A tone of self-deprecation and absurdism rarely seen in corporate internal comms, deployed across Viva Engage, emailing and physical signage.

## Deliverables

- Riddle-format cyber awareness posters
- Bilingual Viva Engage posts FR/EN
- Guerrilla: cafeterias, offices, printers
- "Be Cyber Safe" identity and visuals
- Awareness emailing campaign

## Highlight

> Corporate cybersecurity comms fail because they lecture. Riddles force people to think, which is exactly what phishing resistance requires.
