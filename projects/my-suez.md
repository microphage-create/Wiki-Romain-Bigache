## Short description

Design exploration and pitch for the redesign of My Suez, the corporate intranet of the Suez group (water, waste, energy). Multi-BU, bilingual FR/EN, addressing both office staff and field employees with the content density expected from enterprise tools.

## Context

Suez completed a brand refresh in 2022 (new logo, new colors) and is reviewing its internal digital portal. Microphage was brought in to deliver an alternative design exploration covering:

- IT support portal (ticketing, helpdesk, service catalog)
- HR self-service
- Business apps (HSE / incident reporting, purchasing, travel, training)
- Directory and operational dashboards
- DSI / IT governance tooling

The brief emphasizes a usable interface for **both** office staff and field employees (water engineers, recycling operators, waste technicians), with content density and clarity expected from internal enterprise tools.

## Approach

Brand surcouche of an in-house enterprise design system, applied to the post-2022 Suez visual identity. Tokens, voice and tone, and components ready to deploy on a modern stack (Tailwind v4, Next.js, shadcn/ui-inspired components).

Multi-tenant by design: the same component library serves the four Suez BUs through token overrides, no fork of the component code.

## Status

Pitch in progress. First mockups delivered. Awaiting client decision.
