# L'Ecole Multimedia - Guest Lecturer

| Key | Value |

| **Location** | Paris, France |

## Mission

Guest lecturer during the school's "UX experts week", a recurring program inviting practitioners to share field experience with students.

I ran a UX writing masterclass for students enrolled in the UX design program. The session covered the fundamentals of the craft, the impact of words on user experience, and the interplay between design and content.

## Context

This intervention happened in parallel with my early UX writing missions (Speak UX!, leboncoin), at a moment when UX writing was still a niche specialty in France. The masterclass was one of the first formal exposures to the discipline for these students.
