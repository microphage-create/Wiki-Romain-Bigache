# FDJ - UX Writer

| Key | Value |
|-----|-------|
| **Period** | August 2020 - October 2020 (3 months) |
| **Location** | Paris, France |
| **Industry** | Gambling facilities and casinos |

## Mission

Editorial overhaul of the player account, with a focus on navigation, critical terminology and sensitive flows (certification, withdrawals, betting limits).

## Interface audit and navigation structure

Full interface analysis: information architecture, affordance, label comprehension, terminology consistency. Recommendations on account architecture, simplified entry points, clearer CTAs. Harmonization of key pages around a clear, guided usage logic.

## Terminology and sensitive flows

Overhaul of transaction terminology: deposit, withdrawal, transfer, digital wallet. Rewriting of content related to player certification: ID document, IBAN, verifications. Work on betting limits: hierarchy of critical info, clear and accountability-driven tone.

## Co-design and guidelines

Close collaboration with product and design teams. Concrete recommendations on system messages, help bubbles, feedback states and supporting content. Integration of a clear, educational and reassuring tone of voice.
