## What it is

Les Siestes Electroniques is an international festival dedicated to adventurous music, born in Toulouse in 2002 and touring the world since 2007. The lineup mixes experimental electronic, contemporary classical, world music, and unclassifiable artists.

I was a member of the structure from 2011 to 2018, contributing on the periphery of the programming and the production. Same period and same scene as the Cabanon Records side project.

## What it brought

- Direct exposure to international curation work (artist booking, scenography, contextual programming)
- Hands-on understanding of how a small festival sustains itself outside mainstream music industry circuits
- Network in the experimental music scene, in France and abroad
