## What it was

Le Cabanon Records was an independent experimental music label. Co-founded in 2011, ran for about 7 years before going dormant.

The label released 13+ records: noise, ambient, drone, improv, weird stuff at the edge of the underground music scene. Each release was a curated artisanal object: limited runs, hand-stamped sleeves, screen-printed covers, music that wouldn't fit anywhere else.

## What it taught

- Curating: defining a coherent line across many artists
- Producing: turning a project into a physical object
- Communicating: writing copy for a niche audience without pretension
- Persisting: sustaining a creative endeavor over years on the side of paid work

These reflexes carried directly into Microphage years later: same instinct of shipping things rather than talking about them.
