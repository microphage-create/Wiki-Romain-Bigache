## Mission

Volunteer mentor at Les Entretiens de l'Excellence, a French nonprofit promoting social mobility through mentoring sessions hosted at top French schools (Ecole polytechnique, HEC, Sciences Po, etc.).

I intervened twice (2022 and 2024) on the Ecole polytechnique campus, in the "Metiers du numerique" track. The format: small-group sessions with high school students from priority neighborhoods (REP/REP+), sharing how digital careers actually work, what the day-to-day looks like, what paths are realistic for them.

## Why it matters

The program aims to break the self-censorship loop where students from underprivileged backgrounds rule out top schools or technical careers because nobody around them does it. The mentors are practitioners who can show that the path is real and walkable.
