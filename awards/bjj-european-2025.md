## Context

Silver medal at the IBJJF European Championship in Lisbon, January 2025. Master 1 division (30-35 years old), -76 kg, Novice belt level.

The IBJJF European Championship is the largest BJJ event in Europe and one of the four "majors" of the international BJJ circuit. Several thousand competitors from 50+ countries compete every year.

## Practice

- Training 6x / week
- Started BJJ around 2019, competing since 2023
- Same gym in Paris since the start

## Why it shows up here

Putting it on a CV / LinkedIn isn't about flexing a sport medal. It's a marker of consistency: 6+ years of practice, 6 trainings a week, integrated into a freelance schedule with grand-comptes deliveries.

The main carry-over into the work: stamina under pressure, comfort with sustained discomfort, and a habit of getting tested by reality on a tight schedule. When something has to ship in 3 weeks, the body remembers it has done worse.
