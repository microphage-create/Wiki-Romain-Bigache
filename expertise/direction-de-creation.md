# Creative Direction

Copywriting, art direction, advertising and brand communications for enterprise accounts. 50+ campaigns shipped end-to-end since 2017.

## Background

Trained as a copywriter at Sup de Creation (Paris), end-of-studies internship at Havas Paris (Orange Bank, EDF, leboncoin, La Poste). Two years of freelance copywriting / art direction for Paris agencies (out-of-home, print, radio, social, digital). Then ongoing partnership with [OXGEN](../experience/oxgen.md) since 2017.

## Partnership with OXGEN

OXGEN is a consulting and creative agency for Change Communications at enterprise accounts. Recurring partner since 2017, not employer. They bring me onto enterprise CIO office programs as Creative Director: end-to-end concept, copy, art direction, internal social, executive committee assets.

The same channel also distributes my AI builds (Altaria delivered to Altarea through OXGEN).

## Scope per mission

End-to-end ownership, one-person craft:

- **Strategic concept** for the program
- **Copywriting** (long + short copy, scripts, headlines, taglines)
- **Art direction** (visual system, campaign identity, custom illustration)
- **Internal display** (posters, guerrilla deployment in offices, cafeterias, elevators)
- **Internal social** (Viva Engage, Workplace, Slack, Teams, Yammer)
- **C-suite and Town Hall** decks
- **Training content** and manager toolkits
- **Project management** and coordination of freelance art directors / motion / graphic designers

## Skills applied

- Editorial typography (display, condensed, serif, hand-drawn, kindergarten, halftone)
- Long copy (narratives, manifestos, scripts) and short copy (headlines, taglines, pull quotes)
- Brand voice definition and tone-of-voice guidelines
- Corporate storytelling, twist-reveal hooks, contrarian framing
- Internal social formats (memes, riddles, hijacked codes, sequential teasers)
- Bilingual FR/EN production (native French, fluent English copy)
- Guerrilla marketing and physical-space deployment
- Multi-audience comms (IT teams, business units, C-suite, field workers)
- Change management copy under Compliance and C-suite approval
