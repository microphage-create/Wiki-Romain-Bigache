# Press, third-party testimonials and interviews

External sources about Romain's work. Unlike the blog (self-authored voice, see [writing.md](./writing.md)), these are written or hosted by other people: a client manager who hired him, a UX writing publication. They carry independent validation, which is usually what a recruiter or a prospect is looking for.

## Paul Thanasack (Head of UX, ADEO) on hiring Romain as the group's first UX Writer

Third-party testimonial. Podcast interview by Apolline Rouzé (UX Content Craft), episode #21, April 2021. Paul Thanasack, who ran the UX studio at Leroy Merlin before becoming Head of UX at ADEO, explains why the team brought in a UX Writer and why they picked Romain.

What a recruiter can quote:

- Romain was the **first UX Writer of the ADEO group** (Leroy Merlin), selected through a competitive call against agencies and other freelances.
- Selection criteria, in Paul's words: "his references, his personality and his communication skills".
- The brief was a 2-3 project proof of concept. It worked so well that "everyone wanted to work with him", so it ran for 6 months of projects before being deliberately stopped to refocus on the original goal: training.
- Romain then wrote the editorial guide and built the training program that upskilled the whole UX/UI collective and the business owners.

Same mission documented in [experience/adeo-leroy-merlin.md](./experience/adeo-leroy-merlin.md), here seen from the client's side.
